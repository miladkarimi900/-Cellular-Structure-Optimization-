# Cellular Structure Optimization (CSO)

A novel bio-inspired optimization algorithm based on cellular self-organization principles.

## ?? Overview

Cellular Structure Optimization (CSO) is a population-based metaheuristic algorithm inspired by the self-organization mechanisms of biological cells. The algorithm simulates cellular processes including division, differentiation, and apoptosis to dynamically optimize solutions.

## ?? Features

- **Bio-inspired**: Based on cellular self-organization
- **Adaptive**: Dynamic population management
- **Efficient**: Fast convergence on complex problems
- **Flexible**: Easy to implement and customize
